# Client Intake Checklist

Collect these details before starting work. Do not begin until the scope is clear.

- [ ] Business / brand name and target audience
- [ ] Goal of the content (awareness, promotion, launch, education, etc.)
- [ ] Platform and required dimensions / aspect ratio
- [ ] Exact text, offer details, CTA, and preferred language
- [ ] Brand colors, logo, fonts, and style references
- [ ] Assets supplied by client and confirmation they have usage rights
- [ ] Deliverables, file formats, duration, and deadline
- [ ] Number of concepts and included revision rounds
- [ ] Any restricted claims, legal requirements, or industry rules

## Scope confirmation

Send a written summary of deliverables, timeline, price, and revision limits. Get client approval before production.
