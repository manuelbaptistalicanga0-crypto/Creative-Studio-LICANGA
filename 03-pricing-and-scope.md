# Starter Pricing and Scope Guide

These are suggested test prices, not guaranteed market rates. Adjust after timing several real projects and accounting for platform fees, revisions, licensing, and communication.

| Offer | Starter price | Scope to define before listing |
|---|---:|---|
| Instagram post design | US$10 | One static design, one size, supplied copy, one revision |
| Instagram motion reel | US$25 | One short animation, agreed duration/format, one revision |
| Monthly social media pack | US$75 | Specify exact post count, formats, captions, and revision rounds |

## Pricing rules

1. Calculate estimated production and communication time.
2. Include a buffer for export checks and reasonable revisions.
3. Charge separately for additional concepts, urgent delivery, extra sizes, or extensive edits.
4. Do not promise a turnaround until workload and client inputs are confirmed.
5. Revisit pricing after a useful sample of completed orders; do not rely on impressions alone.
